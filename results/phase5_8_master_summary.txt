INTERPRETATION AND CAVEATS
--------------------------
FedProx with mu=0.01 achieved higher test ROC-AUC than FedAvg in all five tested conditions under best-validation model selection.

This does not establish that FedProx universally outperforms FedAvg. The result applies only to the tested experimental setup.

For strong non-IID, FedProx achieved higher ROC-AUC but lower F1 than FedAvg.

Extreme non-IID contains single-class or nearly single-class clients, so accuracy can be misleading. ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, F1, and client-level distributions should be considered together.

Best-validation-selected results and final-round results must not be mixed when comparing methods.

The later best-validation rounds observed for FedProx under strong and extreme non-IID are observations only and should not be presented as proof of a particular mechanism.

Individual components such as FedAvg, FedProx, Dirichlet partitioning, SHAP, or differential privacy should not be described as novel by themselves. The research contribution should be framed around the integrated framework and its evaluation under healthcare data heterogeneity, subject to literature verification.
